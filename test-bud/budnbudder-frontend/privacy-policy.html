1. Introduction

Bud n' Budder (“Bud n' Budder,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you provide when you visit our website, create an account, browse our products, place an order, contact us, or otherwise interact with our services.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, when we may share it, how we protect it, and the choices available to you.

By accessing or using our website, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with our website, we may collect the following categories of information.

Personal and Account Information

When you create an account or otherwise provide information to us, we may collect:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Account credentials
  • Profile information
  • Information you provide when contacting customer support

Order and Transaction Information

When you place an order, we may collect information necessary to process and fulfill that order, including:

  • Products purchased
  • Quantity and order details
  • Order history
  • Order status
  • Payment-related information
  • Shipping or delivery information
  • Delivery instructions or notes you voluntarily provide

Shipping Information

To fulfill deliveries, we may collect:

  • Name
  • Street address
  • Apartment or unit number
  • City
  • State
  • ZIP/postal code
  • Phone number
  • Other information necessary to complete delivery

Age Verification Information

Because Bud n' Budder sells cannabis products, access to the website is restricted to individuals who meet the applicable legal age requirements.

Our website currently uses an age-verification gate that requires visitors to confirm that they are at least 21 years old before accessing the site.

The current website stores the age-verification status within the browser session so that the visitor does not have to repeatedly confirm their age during the same session.

Technical and Usage Information

When you access our website, certain technical information may be collected automatically by your browser, hosting provider, security systems, or other technology used to operate the website. This may include:

  • IP address
  • Browser type and version
  • Device type
  • Operating system
  • Pages visited
  • Date and time of visits
  • Referring website or page
  • Website interaction information
  • General device or browser information

3. How We Use Your Information

We may use the information we collect to:

  • Create and manage customer accounts
  • Process and fulfill orders
  • Arrange pickup or delivery
  • Communicate with you about your orders
  • Respond to questions and customer-support requests
  • Verify age and comply with applicable cannabis laws and regulations
  • Maintain and improve our website
  • Improve our products and services
  • Detect, prevent, and investigate fraud, abuse, or unauthorized activity
  • Protect the security of our website, customers, employees, and business
  • Maintain business and transaction records
  • Comply with legal and regulatory obligations
  • Send promotional or marketing communications where permitted and where you have provided the appropriate consent

We will only use personal information for purposes that are reasonably related to the purposes described in this Privacy Policy or otherwise permitted or required by law.

4. Cookies & Similar Technologies

Our website may use cookies, browser storage, session storage, and similar technologies. These technologies may be used to:

  • Remember certain preferences
  • Maintain your shopping experience
  • Keep track of your shopping cart
  • Maintain account sessions
  • Remember age-verification status during a browser session
  • Improve website functionality
  • Help protect the website against unauthorized activity

You can manage or restrict cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain technologies may cause portions of the website to function differently or become unavailable.

Our privacy disclosures regarding tracking technologies are intended to accurately describe the technologies actually used on the website. If additional analytics, advertising, or tracking services are added in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated as appropriate.

5. Payment Processing

Payments may be processed through third-party payment providers.

When you make a payment, your payment information may be transmitted directly to the applicable payment processor for authorization and processing.

We do not need to receive or store your full payment-card number when payment information is handled directly by a third-party payment processor.

Payment providers may collect and process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

6. Order & Delivery Information

We use information associated with your order to process, prepare, communicate, and fulfill your purchase.

This may require sharing relevant information with service providers involved in payment processing, delivery, shipping, technology, customer support, fraud prevention, or other services necessary to complete your order.

We only intend to provide third parties with information reasonably necessary for them to perform the services they provide to us.

7. Account Information

If you create an account, we may maintain information associated with that account, including your name, email address, profile information, account activity, and order history.

You are responsible for keeping your account credentials confidential and for notifying us if you believe your account has been accessed without authorization.

We may take reasonable steps to protect account information and prevent unauthorized access.

8. Information You Provide to Us

You may voluntarily provide information through:

  • Account registration
  • Checkout forms
  • Delivery instructions
  • Contact forms
  • Customer-support communications
  • Other forms or communications available through our website

Please avoid providing unnecessary sensitive personal information through free-text fields or customer-support messages.

9. How We Share Information

We may share personal information when reasonably necessary to operate our business, including with:

  • Payment processors
  • Delivery and shipping providers
  • Website hosting and technology providers
  • Security and fraud-prevention providers
  • Customer-support providers
  • Professional advisers
  • Government authorities or law-enforcement agencies when required or permitted by law
  • Other service providers acting on our behalf

We do not authorize service providers to use your information for purposes unrelated to the services they provide to us, except where otherwise permitted by law.

We may also disclose information when necessary to protect our rights, property, customers, employees, or the safety and security of our business.

10. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including to:

  • Complete transactions
  • Maintain customer and business records
  • Provide customer support
  • Resolve disputes
  • Prevent fraud
  • Maintain security
  • Comply with legal, tax, accounting, regulatory, and other obligations

The specific retention period may vary depending on the type of information and the reason it was collected.

When information is no longer reasonably required, we may delete, anonymize, or securely dispose of it where appropriate.

11. Data Security

We take reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, loss, or destruction.

However, no website, electronic transmission, or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

If we become aware of a security incident affecting personal information, we will take appropriate steps consistent with applicable law, which may include investigating the incident, taking corrective measures, and providing required notifications.

12. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have certain rights concerning your personal information, including the right to:

  • Request access to personal information we maintain about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of certain personal information
  • Ask questions about how your information is collected or used
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent
  • Opt out of certain marketing communications
  • Request information about certain disclosures of your personal information

Some requests may be subject to legal limitations or exceptions. To make a privacy request, contact us using the contact information provided at the end of this Privacy Policy. We may need to verify your identity before completing certain requests.

13. Marketing Communications

If you choose to receive promotional communications from us, we may use your contact information to send information about products, promotions, updates, or other communications.

You can unsubscribe from promotional emails by using the unsubscribe mechanism included in the message or by contacting us.

Even if you opt out of marketing communications, we may still send transactional or service-related communications, such as order confirmations, delivery updates, account notices, or security messages.

14. Children's Privacy

Our website is intended for adults who meet the applicable legal age requirements for accessing and purchasing cannabis products. Our website is not intended for individuals under 21 years of age.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under 21 for the purpose of purchasing cannabis products. If we learn that we have collected personal information from an individual who is not legally permitted to use the service, we will take appropriate steps to delete the information where required by law.

15. Cannabis & Age Restrictions

Bud n' Budder operates as an adult-use cannabis retailer and restricts access to individuals who meet applicable legal age requirements.

By using the website, you represent that you meet the applicable legal age requirement in your jurisdiction. Cannabis products may only be purchased, possessed, delivered, or used where permitted by applicable law.

We may collect and use information necessary to verify age, process lawful transactions, fulfill orders, prevent fraud, and comply with applicable cannabis laws and regulations.

Third-party services may have their own privacy policies and terms. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party websites that we do not operate.

Our website is operated from the United States.

If you access the website from outside the United States, your information may be transferred to, stored in, or processed in the United States or other locations where our service providers operate. Privacy protections in those locations may differ from those in your jurisdiction.

Where required by applicable law, we will take appropriate measures concerning international transfers of personal information.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our website, business practices, technology, legal requirements, or services.

When we make changes, we will update the “Last Updated” date at the beginning of this Privacy Policy. We encourage you to review this page periodically to stay informed about how we handle personal information.
